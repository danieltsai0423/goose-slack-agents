## Checked and passing

- No horizontal scroll at 375 px; Sign in stays reachable (the connection menu is hidden on phones by design).
- Dark theme contrast for body, muted and status text (6.04–10.75:1).
- Removed focus outlines are replaced (composer border on focus, background on folder and permission items).
- No banned buzzwords, generic CTAs, fake stats, testimonials, trust claims or filler names in the UI or READMEs.
  ("Unlock" appears only as the game term for opening a floor.)
- `html lang` follows the language switch; the office canvas has a text alternative.

## Proposed fixes

1–2. Rewrite with colons, commas or full stops; list items become "**Label:** text".
3. Update the description to use a colon.
4. Add light-theme colours for those four elements and recheck each pair at 4.5:1 or more.
5. Add one visually hidden `<h1>Goose Office</h1>`.
6. Give top-bar controls a 44 px hit area on touch screens without changing the desktop look.
7. Rotate a turntable only while its card is hovered or focused.
8. Record both reasons in `AGENTS.md`.
9. Show "…" while loading and "0" or "無" when there is nothing.
10. Replace the separators with one plain comment line each.
